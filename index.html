Privacy Policy – Quac Quac Discord Bot

Last Updated: October 16, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Quac Quac Discord Bot (the “Bot”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users interact with it on Discord.

By using the Bot, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features used, the Bot may process the following information:

  • Discord User IDs: To identify users and associate balances, profiles, game records, and other data with the correct Discord account.
  • Discord Usernames: To display player names in balances, transactions, game messages, leaderboards, and records.
  • Server and Channel IDs: To operate channel-based games and restore active games after the Bot restarts.
  • Message Content: The Bot processes message content when users invoke Bot commands, including command arguments and game inputs. The Bot does not intentionally read or store unrelated messages for profiling or advertising.
  • Command and Interaction Data: Command arguments, button interactions, modal submissions, and other information needed to respond to requests.
  • Economy Data: Virtual currency balances, transactions performed through Bot features, daily claim records, shop purchases, and jackpot-related data.
  • Profile Data: Optional About Me text, profile backgrounds, marriage tags, marriage relationships, and related inventory data.
  • Game Data: Game participation, bets, pots, cards, game states, results, scores, statistics, and history for supported games, including Hangman, Russian Roulette Poker, and other Bot games.
  • Server Configuration and Runtime Data: Settings and temporary records required to operate server-specific features.

The Bot does not intentionally collect passwords, Discord account credentials, payment card details, or private messages unrelated to Bot commands and interactions.

3. How We Use Information

Information is used only to operate, maintain, and improve the Bot, including to:

  • Respond to commands, buttons, and other interactions.
  • Maintain virtual currency balances and process supported transactions.
  • Save and restore games when the Bot restarts or temporarily goes offline.
  • Display profiles, leaderboards, game results, and player statistics.
  • Prevent invalid transactions, duplicate actions, abuse, or unauthorized use of owner-only features.
  • Diagnose errors, maintain reliability, and protect the Bot and its users.

The Bot does not use this information to sell advertising or build a profile for unrelated commercial purposes.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Bot data may be stored in local files, databases, or other infrastructure controlled by the Bot operator. For the current deployment, economy, profile, and active game data may be stored in local JSON files controlled by the Bot operator.

Data is retained for as long as necessary to provide the relevant Bot features, maintain game and economy integrity, or comply with applicable obligations. Temporary game data may be removed after a game ends or expires. Historical or account-linked data may remain until it is no longer needed or a valid deletion request is processed.

Because the Bot may need to persist balances and active games, restarting or stopping the Bot does not necessarily delete stored data.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or rent users’ personal information.

Information may be disclosed only:

  • To Discord and its services as necessary for the Bot to operate through the Discord platform.
  • To infrastructure or service providers used to host or maintain the Bot, where applicable.
  • When required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect the Bot, its operator, users, or the public from fraud, abuse, security threats, or harm.

The Bot does not intentionally share account-linked data publicly except where the relevant feature displays it in a Discord server, such as a profile, leaderboard, transaction message, or game result.

6. Public and User-Visible Information

Some information may be visible to other users when a feature is used. This can include:

  • Discord display names or usernames.
  • Public profile information and About Me text.
  • Virtual currency balances or transaction results shown by commands.
  • Leaderboard rankings.
  • Game participation, cards, results, and scores shown in a game channel.
  • Marriage tags and related profile information.

Users should avoid entering sensitive or confidential information into public profile fields, commands, or game interactions.

7. Profile Cards and Third-Party Assets

When generating a profile card, the Bot may retrieve publicly available Discord avatar images and custom emoji assets from Discord’s CDN. These assets are used to display the requested card and are not stored as a separate user profile database by the Bot.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect stored data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. Access to Bot data should be limited to the Bot operator and authorized administrators where appropriate.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

9. User Rights and Data Deletion

Users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of data associated with their Discord account, subject to applicable law and the Bot’s operational requirements.

To submit a request, contact the Bot developer through the official support server or the contact information provided on the Bot’s profile. A request should include the user’s Discord ID and enough information to identify the relevant data.

Some data may need to be retained temporarily to prevent fraud, resolve disputes, preserve economy or game integrity, or comply with legal obligations.

10. Children’s Privacy

The Bot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law or Discord’s policies. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the Bot, contact the Bot developer so the matter can be reviewed.

11. Third-Party Services and Discord

The Bot operates through Discord. Your use of Discord and the Bot is also subject to Discord’s Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

The Bot may use third-party hosting or infrastructure services to operate. Those services may process data only as needed to provide hosting, storage, monitoring, or other operational support.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the Bot, its features, or applicable requirements.

The latest version will be published at the same location. Continued use of the Bot after an update constitutes acknowledgment of the revised policy where permitted by applicable law.

13. Contact

For questions, privacy-related requests, or data deletion requests, contact the Bot developer through the official support server or the contact information listed on the Bot’s Discord profile.
